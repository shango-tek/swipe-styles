# Swiss Grid

Big condensed grotesque type on white, a strict grid, black rules and one accent color.
A bar at the top of every slide fills up as the reader swipes, so they always know how far they are.

**Suits:** professional tips, LinkedIn explainers, frameworks, business lessons, posts built on numbers.
**Not for:** cosy, playful or handmade topics. Use Notebook or Riso Zine for those.

## Writing for this style

- Headlines are short statements: 3 to 8 words. They are set very large. A longer headline gets smaller
  automatically, but it loses the poster effect.
- `kicker`: a small bold label above the headline on the cover, text, points and stat slides: 1 to 4 words.
- `points`: 3 to 5 items. Give each item a short `title` (2 to 5 words) and one sentence of `text`, 18 words
  per item at most. Items are separated by black rules and are not numbered, so they also work for lists
  that are not steps.
- `stat`: only the number goes in `value` ("38%", "4.2×", "12k"); what it means goes in `label` (up to 8 words).
- `quote`: up to 30 words, with `author` and `role` under it.
- `image`: the picture fills the top of the slide and is cropped to fit. Photos and screenshots with one
  clear subject work best.
- `cta`: the `action` sits in an accent block: up to 5 words.
- `*word*` turns a word into the accent color. Use it once in the whole carousel at most.

## Slides

| type | looks like |
|---|---|
| `cover` | kicker and logo at the top, the headline huge at the bottom, a black rule, the subtitle |
| `text` | kicker, headline, then one paragraph, at the bottom of the slide |
| `points` | kicker, headline, then the items separated by black rules |
| `quote` | a large accent quotation mark hanging in the margin, the quote, author and role |
| `stat` | kicker, the number very large, an accent bar, the label and a note |
| `image` | the image fills the top of the slide, title and caption below |
| `cta` | inverted, white on black; logo, headline, text, and the `action` in an accent block |

## Brand

`brand.accent` replaces the red (#E30613). It colors the progress bar, the quotation mark, the stat bar,
`*emphasis*` and the call-to-action block. Pick a strong, saturated color: the call-to-action block puts
white text on it, and on the last slide it sits on black, where very dark accents (navy, dark green) look
muted. Pale colors don't work: emphasized words must stay readable on white. `brand.logo` appears at the top
of the cover and the last slide. `brand.handle` appears in the footer of every slide, with the counter.
