# Swiss Grid

Big condensed grotesque type on white, a strict grid, black rules and one accent color.
A bar at the top of every slide fills up as the reader swipes, so they always know how far they are.

**Suits:** professional tips, LinkedIn explainers, frameworks, business lessons, posts built on numbers.
**Not for:** cosy, playful or handmade topics. Use Notebook or Riso Zine for those.

## Writing for this style

- Headlines are short statements: 3 to 8 words. They are set very large. A longer headline gets smaller
  automatically, but it loses the poster effect.
- One idea per slide.
- `points`: 3 to 5 items. Give each item a short `title` (2 to 5 words) and one sentence of `text`.
  Items are separated by black rules and are not numbered, so they also work for lists that are not steps.
- `stat`: only the number goes in `value` ("38%", "4.2×", "12k"); what it means goes in `label`.
- `quote`: up to 30 words, with `author` and `role`.
- `*word*` turns a word into the accent color. Use it once in the whole carousel at most.

## Slides

| type | looks like |
|---|---|
| `cover` | kicker at the top, the headline huge at the bottom, a black rule, the subtitle |
| `text` | headline, then one paragraph |
| `points` | headline, then the items separated by black rules |
| `quote` | a large accent quotation mark hanging in the margin, the quote, author and role |
| `stat` | the number very large, an accent bar, the label and a note |
| `image` | the image fills the top of the slide, title and caption below |
| `cta` | inverted, white on black; the `action` sits in an accent block |

## Brand

`brand.accent` replaces the red. Pick a strong color that white text can sit on: the call-to-action block
uses white text on the accent. `brand.logo` appears at the top of the cover and the last slide.
