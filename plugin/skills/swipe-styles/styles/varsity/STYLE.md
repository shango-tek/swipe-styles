# Varsity

A letterman jacket in slides: navy wool, big block letters in tackle twill (a white letter with a gold
ring and an outer ring), sleeve stripes, felt pennants and chenille patches, and a knit waistband along the
bottom of every slide. It feels like game day: loud, proud and made for a team.

**Suits:** fitness, running and sports, coaching, team building, school and college, game-day content.
**Not for:** delicate, quiet or sad topics, and anything that needs a calm, private tone. Use Notebook for those.

## Writing for this style

- Headlines: 2 to 6 words (the cover up to 7, the points slide up to 5). They are set in capitals in a
  collegiate block face, huge on the cover, stat and last slide, so short punchy words work best. A word is
  never split: a very long word makes the whole headline smaller. On the cover, a hyphenated word
  ("warm-up") may break at its hyphen; force the lines with `\n` ("5 warm-up\nmoves before\nyour run").
- `kicker`: a felt pennant above the headline on the cover, text, points and stat slides, in capitals:
  1 to 4 words.
- `subtitle` (cover) up to 16 words; `body` (text) up to 50 words; `text` on the last slide up to 16 words.
- `points`: 3 to 5 items, drawn as a playbook: the markers alternate ◯ and ✕ like a play diagram and are
  joined by a dashed route line. Give each item a short `title` (1 to 3 words, set in capitals) and one
  short instruction as `text`: 12 words per item in all. Plain strings work too and are set larger.
- `stat`: `value` is a jersey number, 1 to 5 characters ("5–10", "26.2", "90%", "3×"). The `label` sits on
  a scoreboard panel in capitals: up to 7 words. The `note` (up to 18 words) explains the number or says it
  is a rule of thumb.
- `quote`: up to 26 words, on the cream slide under a big twill quotation mark. `author` goes on a felt
  ribbon in capitals; `role` is a small line under it. The creator's own words with the brand name as
  `author` work well.
- `image`: the picture sits in a felt patch with a stitched edge and is cropped to fill a near-square frame,
  so keep the subject in the middle. Action shots, gear and team photos work; text near the picture's edges
  may be cut off. `title` up to 6 words, `caption` up to 16.
- `cta`: `action` is printed on a cream game ticket with a star stub: up to 4 words ("Save this post",
  "Join Saturday's run").
- `*word*`: in a headline the letters turn into the team color with a white ring, like the second color of
  a two-color jersey number; in body text the word turns team color and bold; in the quote it gets a thick
  team-color underline. Use it once or twice in the whole carousel.

## Brand

`brand.accent` replaces the athletic gold (#F6B31A). It is the team color: the ring around every twill
letter, the sleeve stripes and the waistband, the pennant, the patch rims, the author ribbon, the scoreboard
frame, the ticket stub and `*emphasis*`. The ground stays navy, so team colors that go with navy look best:
gold, orange, red, light blue, royal blue, teal. Every color stays readable: dark accents are lifted until
they show on navy (a navy or maroon brand color becomes a lighter tint of itself), emphasized words use an
even lighter tint, and the text on the pennant and ribbon turns navy on light team colors and white on dark
ones. Very pale colors (cream, pale yellow) work but the ring then blends into the white letters and the
two-color look gets weaker. `brand.logo` appears on a round cream patch with a stitched edge, at the top
right of the cover and above the headline of the last slide; dark or colorful logos on a transparent
background work best, white logos disappear on the cream. `brand.handle` appears at the bottom left of every
slide, with the counter in jersey digits at the bottom right.
