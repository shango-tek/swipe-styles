# Riso Zine

A two-color Risograph print: fluorescent pink and medium blue ink on grainy paper, with yellow as a third ink.
Where inks overlap they darken, like real overprinting. Headlines are chunky and printed slightly out of
register, with the pink plate peeking out behind the blue.

**Suits:** art and design, music, events, creative tips, zines and DIY, anything for a young creative audience.
**Not for:** serious finance, health or formal announcements. Use Swiss Grid for those.

## Writing for this style

- Short and punchy. Headlines: 2 to 6 words. The type is huge and condensed.
- `kicker` sits on a strip of yellow tape: 1 to 3 words ("Weekend project", "New drop").
- `points`: 3 to 5 items with pink stars. Items can be plain text (one short line) or `title` + `text`.
- `stat`: `value` is printed huge in pink. Keep it short: "120", "3×", "48h".
- `quote`: up to 22 words.
- `image`: the picture is printed as a pink duotone with a blue frame offset behind it. Photos with clear
  shapes and contrast work best; details in dark areas get lost.
- `*word*` prints a word in pink. Use it sparingly.

## Slides

| type | looks like |
|---|---|
| `cover` | yellow sun and pink halftone circle, a huge off-register headline at the bottom |
| `text` | headline, a paragraph, a blue moon rising in the corner |
| `points` | headline, items with pink stars, halftone dots in the corner |
| `quote` | a giant blue quotation mark over a big halftone circle |
| `stat` | the number huge in pink with the blue plate off register, over a yellow sun |
| `image` | the image as a pink duotone with an offset blue frame |
| `cta` | yellow paper, the action on a tilted blue sticker |

## Brand

`brand.accent` replaces the fluorescent pink. A bright, light color works best (it is the second ink and
the duotone color); dark colors make the image slide too dark.
