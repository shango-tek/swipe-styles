# Notebook

A page from a squared school notebook: printed serif text, a yellow highlighter across the headline, and
notes, ticks and circles drawn in blue ink. It feels like a friend's class notes.

**Suits:** study tips, teaching, coaching, how-to guides, book notes, self-improvement.
**Not for:** corporate announcements or hard sales. Use Swiss Grid for those.

## Writing for this style

- Write the way a good teacher talks: plain, warm, direct. Short sentences.
- Headlines: 3 to 7 words, up to 6 on the last slide. The cover and last-slide headlines are highlighted line by line.
- `kicker` is a handwritten note in blue ink above the headline ("Study notes", "Week 3"). Keep it to 1 to 4 words.
- `points`: 3 or 4 items, each with a ticked box. Each item: a short `title` and one sentence of `text`.
  The ticks mean "do this", so the items should be actions. 16 words per item at most.
- `quote`: up to 22 words. It is handwritten on a sticky note, so it must be short.
- `stat`: `value` is written by hand and circled. Short values work best ("24 of 30", "3×", "15 min").
  `label` says what it means, up to 10 words.
- `image`: the picture is taped onto the page; `caption` is a handwritten note under it.
- `cta`: `action` is handwritten, with an arrow pointing to it ("Save this for exam week"): up to 5 words.
- `*word*` gets a wavy ink underline. Once or twice per carousel at most.

## Slides

| type | looks like |
|---|---|
| `cover` | handwritten kicker and the logo at the top, a heavy highlighted headline, the subtitle on the grid lines |
| `text` | handwritten kicker, headline with an ink squiggle under it, then a paragraph on the grid lines |
| `points` | handwritten kicker, headline, then items with hand-ticked boxes |
| `quote` | the quote handwritten on a taped yellow sticky note |
| `stat` | handwritten kicker, the number in ink with a hand-drawn circle, then the label and a note |
| `image` | a photo taped onto the page, a printed title and a handwritten caption |
| `cta` | the logo, a highlighted headline, the text, and the action handwritten next to an ink arrow |

## Brand

`brand.accent` replaces the blue ink (#1D3F9E). It colors all the handwriting and the hand-drawn marks:
ticks, circles, squiggles, underlines and arrows. Pick a dark, ink-like color (navy, dark green, burgundy):
it is used for handwriting on white paper and on the yellow sticky note, where light colors become
unreadable. `brand.logo` appears at the top of the cover and the last slide. `brand.handle` appears in the
footer of every slide, with the page number.
